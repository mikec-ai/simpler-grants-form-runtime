# Outcome

Converge the current declarative kernel and Billy's PR #44 into one portable, contract-first architecture that visibly composes reusable questions, compiles through generic adapters, preserves exact source and review evidence, derives the analytical deliverable, and makes each additional form cheaper to add.

# Sequencing

The roadmap first inventories and preserves the existing form corpus, hardens the provisional contract and evidence boundary, proves it across producers and consumers, stress-tests a complex budget family, records one canonical authoring decision, consolidates the repository, and only then scales migration in overlap order. Architecture questions stay explicit and may be resolved through implementation evidence rather than assigned to a particular person.

# Success criteria

- One versioned portable artifact contract supports TypeSpec and JSON authoring.
- No form-specific semantic decisions live in compiler or adapter code.
- Reuse is visible through the canonical graph and role-qualified occurrence bindings.
- The same artifacts work in Simpler and an independent consumer.
- Existing schema, UI, rule, XML, and behavior evidence survives migration.
- Analysis is generated from implementation evidence with proposed, accepted, and published states separated.
- Large oracles and analysis outputs are generated and published by CI rather than checked into runtime source.
- One canonical authoring source, one portable contract, and one Simpler adapter remain after consolidation.
- No superseded PR or branch is closed or deleted until its unique source, oracle, mapping, and test evidence has a recorded destination.
